Happy Birthday, My Love ❤️

“Above all, keep loving each other deeply, because love covers over a multitude of sins.”

— 1 Peter 4:8 ✨

Happy Birthday to my husband, my love, and my safest place. ❤️

You are the comfort zone in my life, the person with whom I can truly be myself. The love, care, patience, and support you give me feel like a beautiful gift from God. Sometimes I wonder how I got so lucky to have you, because you feel like the answer to so many of my prayers. 🤍

The day we came together in God's presence, something in my heart decided that I never want to leave your side. The promises I made to you were never just words. They came from the deepest part of my heart, and I mean every single one of them.

We may fight. You may get angry. We may have difficult days and moments when we don't understand each other. But no matter what happens, I will never leave you. I want to stand beside you not only on the happy days, but also through every difficult moment life brings us. ❤️

This is your first birthday with me, and even though we haven't had all that much time together, you have already given me so many beautiful memories, so much love, and so much support. In such a short time, you've become such an important part of my life, and I can't imagine my life without you in it.

On your special day, my biggest prayer is that God blesses you with a long, happy, healthy, peaceful, and prosperous life. May He guide you in everything you do, protect you wherever you go, and fill your life with endless happiness and success. 🙏🏻✨

And my selfish little prayer is that God gives us many, many more birthdays to celebrate together— side by side, growing old together, making countless memories, laughing over silly things, surviving our little fights, and loving each other through it all. 🥹❤️

Happy Birthday, my love. Thank you for being my comfort, my happiness, my support, and my home.

I love you today, tomorrow, and through every birthday that is yet to come. ❤️🎂✨

Forever yours. ❤️

— Monica ❤️
